Syllables are separated by spaces and hyphens. _ holds the last syllable over the next note. [dh uh] gives a syllable as sounds (consonants s z sh ch j f v th dh h t d k g p b m n ng l r w y, vowels a ah ae e eh i ih o oh u oo uh er aw). # starts a comment. Rests take no syllable. Too few syllables and the last notes sing "la"; too many and the extra are not sung.

The preview and the WAV are the same samples the command line writes. space plays and stops, click the waveform to seek. The song file is what --out-json gives: fix a syllable or a chord by hand and sing it again from that.
On the real song: a synthesized cover does not need the recording's master licence, but the composition is still under copyright, and so is a transcription of it, so it still needs a synchronisation licence from the song's publishers before it goes in the trailer. Keep the MIDI, the recording, the lyrics file and the song file of any copyrighted song out of the repository.
