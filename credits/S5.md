# Credits rows from the road out front (for CREDITS.md)

Three more Sketchfab models, all [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (the licence checked against the Sketchfab API by `tools/assets/fetch_sketchfab.py` before each download), fetched and slimmed into `assets/props/` from the rows in `tools/assets/manifests/S5.json` (`python3 tools/assets/fetch_sketchfab.py --manifest tools/assets/manifests/S5.json --credits` prints these lines). Add them to the Sketchfab list beside the other cars:

  - `car_lt80`: [Fairheaven LT '80 - Low poly model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/e2678da920cc4be68dbc193727919ffb) by [DanielZhabotinsky](https://sketchfab.com/DanielZhabotinsky), CC BY 4.0 (a car on the road out front, repainted in the game)
  - `car_kiri`: [Kiri '86 - Low poly model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5c17c582ef9549798f694b660feea42a) by [DanielZhabotinsky](https://sketchfab.com/DanielZhabotinsky), CC BY 4.0 (a car on the road out front, repainted in the game)
  - `car_wreck`: [Abandoned Generic Sedan 2 - Game Ready](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/db2df46c49f9441286c84c66208ef7ac) by [rashad-brahimli](https://sketchfab.com/rashad-brahimli), CC BY 4.0 (the wreck in the wood)

The silo is built in the game (no model to credit): its staves take the concrete set and its roof the metal set already listed under the PBR textures.
