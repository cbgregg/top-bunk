# Credits rows from the barn (for CREDITS.md)

One model, from Poly Haven, CC0 (public domain; credited anyway). It was downloaded at the 1k glTF resolution through the Poly Haven API
(`https://api.polyhaven.com/files/covered_car`) and slimmed by `tools/assets/slim_models.mjs --in covered_car_1k.gltf --out
assets/props/covered_car.glb --tris 5000 --tex 512` (12 592 to 5 000 triangles, three 512 px WebP textures, 152 KB). Everything else in
the barn (the walls' colliders, the sliding door and its rail, the plank floor's dirt, the stalls, the loft and its ladder, the hay, the
tarps over the Hollis furniture, the crib, the bike, the dog chain, the mattress, the drawings, the measuring post, the feed sack, the
sawhorses, the work lamp and the field gate's leaf) is built in code in `js/barn.js` and carries no licence. The barn shell itself is the
existing `barn.glb` (American Old Barn by andrewriddle, CC BY 4.0, already in CREDITS.md); the wallet photo reuses the existing
`assets/photos/boy.webp` (Library of Congress, no known restrictions, already in `assets/photos/credits.json`).
