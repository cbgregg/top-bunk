# Words for tools/sing.js, one syllable per melody note, in order. "Little Window", the demo lullaby in robot_song.json.
#
# Syllables are separated by spaces and hyphens: "lit-tle win-dow" is four notes. Rests in the melody take no syllable.
# "_" holds the last syllable over the next note (a tie, or the vowel again on a new pitch). A "-" on its own is skipped.
# A syllable in square brackets is given as sounds instead of spelling, for words the spelling would get wrong:
# [dh uh] is "the", [n ah i t] is "night". The sounds are listed at the top of tools/sing.js.
# Everything after a "#" is a comment.
